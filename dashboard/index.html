Forecasting kharif yield
from rainfall

Telangana's thirty-two districts, kharif 2014-15 to 2022-23, four crops: cotton, arhar, moong and rice. Yields come from the Directorate of Economics & Statistics; daily rainfall comes from ERA5 reanalysis via the Open-Meteo archive API. The 2026 figures are model predictions from each crop's own selected model — and every one of them carries its out-of-sample skill score, because a prediction without one is not worth reading.

Yield by year, and the 2026 forecast

Each crop is forecast by its own model — the one that survived cross-validation inside the fitting period and produced valid yields at 2026's rainfall. Every crop is indexed to its own Telangana average (2016-17 → 2022-23 = 100), so crops measured in different units share one axis; the dashed segment and the diamond are the 2026 prediction.

Does the rainfall optimum depend on the season window?

Jun–Sep is the sowing window, not the growing season. The same model is refitted on Jul–Sep and Jun–Oct rainfall to test whether the conclusion survives a different window.
